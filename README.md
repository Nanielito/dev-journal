# Dev Journal

> Experiments, engineering decisions, and lessons learned while building software.

A personal development journal for exploring tools, testing ideas, documenting technical decisions, and sharing what I learn along the way.

This isn't a collection of perfect tutorials. Some experiments succeed, some don't, and both are worth documenting.

## Contents

- [Experiments](experiments/) — Setup notes, methodology, findings, and tradeoffs.
- [Articles](articles/) — Publication-ready writing based on experiments and engineering experience.
- [Assets](assets/) — Screenshots, diagrams, and other supporting media.

## Principles

- Learn by building.
- Explain the tradeoffs, not just the outcome.
- Record reproducible steps and relevant tool versions.
- Document mistakes and unexpected results.
- Keep the workflow simple until complexity is justified.

## Organization

Experiments are working notes, not necessarily polished articles. Articles are edited narratives that may draw on one or more experiments. Supporting screenshots and diagrams live in `assets/` or alongside the relevant experiment when they are experiment-specific.
